About SoundFlow

Official Audio & Video Discovery

SoundFlow is an educational and music discovery platform designed with pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.

Legal Principles & Compliance

• All playback is executed via the official YouTube IFrame Player API.
• External tracks and artists can be opened directly on Spotify.
• SoundFlow does NOT convert to MP3, download audio files, scrape audio streams, or circumvent DRM.

Computer Science Architecture

Constructed with a modular, lightweight architecture: storage engine, API fetcher, player controller, and responsive view system.
